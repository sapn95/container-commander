Turn on watching

container commander cannot see navigation yet, so it will never decide anything — policy or no policy.

Firefox will ask to access your data for all websites. That is how an add-on is allowed to hold a request long enough to decide which container it belongs in — a tab cannot be moved once it has loaded. It reads no page content and sends nothing anywhere: what it looks at, in full. Switching it off again revokes this.
