Three extensions by the same author, on the same toolbar

Actual pixels at 16×16 — the size that decides whether an icon works.
Left to right in every bar: beeline, linkward, container commander.

before

linkward and commander shared the same gradient, byte for byte, and the same white-and-amber ink. Commander’s three 1.8 px lanes merged into a comb.

after

Separated on hue first, then on silhouette: red squares, a blue stroke, green discs.

One white disc above two dark ones. The unpicked two are darker than the badge, not paler: an empty place is evidence that something acted, where a dimmer lamp is a status indicator — and a status indicator invites the click this extension refuses to honour.
